# 🇮🇳 Indian Travel Example Prompts for BackpackBuddy

Curated prompt examples for testing BackpackBuddy's budget travel concierge, hostel lookups, currency conversion, allergy checks, and media generation tailored to Indian destinations.

---

## 🏰 Budget Hostels & Accommodation
1. **Jaipur / Rajasthan Backpacking**:
   > *"Find budget hostels in Jaipur, India with dorm beds under ₹1,500/night ($18 USD) near Hawa Mahal."*
2. **Goa Beach Hostels**:
   > *"Recommend top-rated backpacker hostels in Anjuna and Arambol, Goa with free Wi-Fi and social common rooms."*
3. **Rishikesh Yoga & Mountain Stays**:
   > *"Search for river-facing hostels and budget guesthouses in Tapovan, Rishikesh under ₹1,200/night."*
4. **Varanasi Heritage Hostels**:
   > *"Find affordable hostels near Dashashwamedh Ghat in Varanasi for a 2-day budget stay."*
